# Quiet LA recovered workspace

The local default restores capabilities from `implementation/outputs/deploy/quiet-la-v2.1-dense/` while simplifying the controls. The earlier policy portal remains available at `?study=pilot` and is the only interface used by external/private profiles.

## Recovered capabilities

- Georeferenced Field, Bands, and Glow rasters; independent Dots display. Surface modes hide receiver dots and columns. A selected receiver retains its inspection marker.
- Lazy-loaded terrain and 33,638 building features in 3D, with camera location and zoom retained when switching modes.
- Two alternative synthetic scenarios, three periods, overview/neighborhood/exact receiver levels, and exact inspection independent of display sampling.
- Saved receiver comparisons and reloadable view state.

## Interface treatment

The map fills the workspace beside one 328 px sidebar. On mobile, controls collapse into one bottom sheet; the map center stays available. Search, period, display, and scenario controls follow a fixed reading order. Model limits and saved comparisons use disclosures. A compact relative-result legend stays on the map. Keyboard focus, radio-key navigation, 44 px mobile controls, and reduced-motion preferences are supported.

This removes the previous overlapping instrument cards and persistent disclosure rail. The portal retains Quiet LA's warm paper, charcoal, indigo, and relative-result color ramp. It does not imply measured noise, current traffic, address prediction, or county-wide coverage.
